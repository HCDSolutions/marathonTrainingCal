Workout Type Descriptions

  • Easy Run: Relaxed, comfortable pace for active recovery and building aerobic base.
  • VO2 Max: Short, high-intensity intervals to improve aerobic capacity.
  • Steady State Run: Sustained, comfortably hard pace to build endurance and efficiency.
  • Progressive Long Run: Long run that gradually increases in pace, finishing strong.
  • Strides and Drills: Short bursts and technique drills to improve speed and running form.
  • Hill Sprints: Short, powerful sprints up a hill to build strength and power.
  • Long Runs: Weekly long-distance runs to build aerobic base and stamina.
  • Zone 2 Runs: Easy, conversational pace runs for aerobic development and recovery.
  • Strength Training: Gym or bodyweight exercises to improve overall strength and injury resistance.
  • Rest Day: No running; focus on recovery and adaptation.
  • Marathon Race: The big day! 26.2 miles at your best effort.
  • Half Marathon Race: Race day! 13.1 miles at your best effort.
